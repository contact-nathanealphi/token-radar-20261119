How the value is figured

Every number on this page is computed from the sealed snapshot by the program that built it — nothing is hand-typed, and no protocol name is special-cased. The valuation is a peer multiple: live protocols tell us what the market pays per dollar of TVL, and a pre-token protocol is priced at its own category's multiple.

implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × seasoning × reach

  • Peer multiple — the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live-token protocols in the same category (at least 3 peers; otherwise the median over every live protocol), clamped to 0.05–2.0.
  • Growth — 1 + the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150%, weighted 0.4.
  • Seasoning — 0.7 rising to 1.0 over two years tracked with no token; an unknown listing date counts as one year.
  • Reach — 1.00 rising 0.03 per extra chain, up to four: more chains, more surface for a launch.
